# Share and export

This file is the piece about the walk as it will go to the parish magazine, with a note to the editor above it. Use it to try the ways of getting text out of WriteFlex. Where a command has no key, its name is in bold: press <kbd>⇧⌘P</kbd>, type the name and press <kbd>↵</kbd>.

> **Copy as.** These copy the whole file, ready to paste somewhere else. **Copy as rich text** keeps the headings and the emphasis, for pasting into Word, an email or Teams. **Copy as Markdown** copies the file as it is written. **Copy as plain text** leaves out all the marks. Choose **Copy as rich text** and paste the note below into an email.

## Note to the editor

Dear Margaret,

Here is the piece for the autumn number, a little under the length you asked for. The distances are from the map and I have walked them twice since to be sure. If there is room for a picture, the second stile is the one to use.

> **Export and presets.** Press <kbd>⌥⌘E</kbd> to open Export. It makes one file to send, as a web page or a Word document, and you choose the design and whether there is a front page and a list of contents. A reader of the web page can add comments or suggest edits in their browser and send them back without an account. In the Files pane, <kbd>⌘K</kbd> on a file's row offers **Export…** for that file without opening it.

## The long way round

The walk is four miles if you go by the road and nearly seven if you go the long way. I have always gone the long way.

The first half mile is all hedgerow. In September the hawthorn is heavy with haws and the blackberries have gone soft and dark. You hear the road for longer than you expect, and then, somewhere past the second stile, you stop hearing it at all.

At the top the land opens out. The fields are large and pale, the walls are dry stone, and the sky takes up most of the view. Every wall up here was built by hand, stone by stone, without mortar.

The path drops through a hanging wood, and the village roofs come back one at a time. It takes twice as long as the road, and I remember four times as much of it.

> **Presets.** A preset is a set of export choices kept under a name, such as Parish magazine, so that you do not make them again each time. Set the choices in Export, then **Save export preset as…** keeps them. **Apply export preset** uses a preset for the next export, and **Update export preset** replaces what it holds with the choices you have now. **Manage export presets** lists them all. **Rename export preset** and **Delete export preset** act on the one selected there, and **Move export preset up** and **Move export preset down** change its place in the list.

> **Sharing.** **Set up sharing** connects WriteFlex to your own Cloudflare account, once on each Mac, so that a piece can later be shared by link. It lists the permissions the Cloudflare token needs, checks the token you paste, keeps it in the macOS Keychain and installs a small sharing service on that account. Settings › Sharing replaces the token, updates the service and removes sharing from the account. Then <kbd>⌥⌘S</kbd>, or the Share mark at the right of the title bar, opens the Sharing page of the open piece. Make a link there, with a name if you like and with readers allowed to leave comments or not, and copy it to send. Each time the piece is saved, the page at that link shows the new text within a few seconds. Turn off stops the link for every reader at once; Turn on brings it back at the same address. **Shares list for this collection**, or Shares beside the collection's name in Files, lists every link in the collection with its piece, name, settings, the day it was made and how many times it was opened. From there you copy a link, change its settings, turn it off or open its piece's Sharing page.
