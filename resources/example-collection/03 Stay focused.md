# Stay focused

This file is a passage to carry on writing. The commands here change what you see while you write, never the text. Where a command has no key, its name is in bold: press <kbd>⇧⌘P</kbd>, type the name and press <kbd>↵</kbd>.

> **Typewriter and distraction-free.** Put the caret at the end of the paragraph under **The second stile** and press <kbd>⌘T</kbd>. The line you are writing now stays at one height and the text moves up past it. **Fixed line: top third**, **Fixed line: middle** and **Fixed line: lower third** set that height. <kbd>⌥⌘D</kbd> hides the panes and bars and leaves only the text; the same key brings them back.

## The second stile

It is a ladder stile, grey with lichen, and someone has cut a notch in the top rail for a dog to get through. From the top step you can see the whole of the first half mile behind you and none of what is ahead.

> **Writing sessions and goals.** Press <kbd>⌃⌥⌘S</kbd> to start a timed session. Type the number of minutes, add a number of words to aim for if you want one, and press <kbd>↵</kbd>. Then write on from the paragraph above: what is on the other side of the stile? **Adjust writing-session target…** changes the number of words while the session runs, and **End focused writing session** stops it early. **Set writing goal…** is separate from sessions: it gives this file a number of words to reach, shown beside the word count at the bottom of the window.

## The field beyond

The path is not marked here. You keep the wall on your left and walk towards the single ash tree, and the ground rises so gradually that you only notice it when you turn round.

> **Focus modes.** **Focus…** opens the list of focus modes; press a number to choose one, or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close the list. Each mode also has its own command. **Fade other paragraphs** dims every paragraph but the one the caret is in. **Highlight sentence** marks the sentence the caret is in. **Segment focus** marks the part of the sentence the caret is in. **Sentence breakup** shows each sentence on a line of its own. **Anchored reading** makes the first letters of each word stand out. Try each on the paragraph under **The ash tree**.

## The ash tree

It stands alone because it grew in a corner the plough could not reach. Rooks use it. In winter you can count the nests, and in summer you can only hear them.

> **Line focus and the Ruler.** **Focus: Line focus** dims the text except the lines around the caret, and **Line focus: 1 line**, **Line focus: 3 lines** and **Line focus: 5 lines** set how many stay clear. **Focus: Ruler** lays a band across the text at the caret. **Ruler: thin rule**, **Ruler: 1 line**, **Ruler: 3 lines**, **Ruler: 5 lines** and **Ruler: all below** set what it covers, and **Ruler opacity: lighter** and **Ruler opacity: darker** change how strong it is. Both follow the caret. **Line focus / Ruler: follow by hand** makes them follow the pointer instead; choosing it again goes back to the caret. **Line focus / Ruler: follow typewriter** does the same for Typewriter, and choosing it again goes back to the caret. Typewriter: the band stays on the typewriter line and the text moves through it.
