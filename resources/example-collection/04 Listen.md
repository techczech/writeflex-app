# Listen

WriteFlex can read a file aloud, marking the sentence and the word as it goes. This file is a passage to listen to. Hearing a draft is a quick way to find the sentence that is too long. Where a command has no key, its name is in bold: press <kbd>⇧⌘P</kbd>, type the name and press <kbd>↵</kbd>.

> **Read aloud.** Put the caret at the start of the paragraph under **Weather** and press <kbd>⌃⌥R</kbd>. Reading starts at the caret; the same key pauses it and carries on. <kbd>⌃⌥⇧R</kbd> stops. **Read aloud** in the palette starts it too.

## Weather

The weather arrives from the west, and on the wold you see it an hour before it reaches you. First the far hills lose their edges. Then the nearer fields go dull, one after another, as if someone were drawing a cloth across them.

There is nowhere to shelter. The walls are waist high and the one ash tree is a field away, so you put your hood up and keep walking, and the rain comes across the grass towards you in a line you could draw with a ruler.

> **Move about while it reads.** <kbd>⌃⌥→</kbd> skips to the next sentence and <kbd>⌃⌥←</kbd> goes back one. <kbd>⌃⌥↓</kbd> and <kbd>⌃⌥↑</kbd> do the same by paragraph. <kbd>⌃⌥.</kbd> reads faster and <kbd>⌃⌥,</kbd> slower. If you scroll away to look at something else, <kbd>⌃⌥J</kbd> returns to the sentence being read.

## After the rain

It is over in twenty minutes. The stone of the walls goes from grey to nearly black and then dries in patches, lightest on the top stones where the wind reaches. Everything smells of wet grass and sheep.
