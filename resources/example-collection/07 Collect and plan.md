# Collect and plan

A folder is a collection, and each file in it is a piece. This file plans the piece about the walk for the parish magazine: its parts, the ideas not yet written and the books behind it. Most of the commands here act on the Board, the Folio outline or the Reading Room, not on this text, so each instruction says where to be. Where a command has no key, its name is in bold: press <kbd>⇧⌘P</kbd>, type the name and press <kbd>↵</kbd>.

> **Collections, Board and Folio outline.** Press <kbd>⇧⌘B</kbd> to open the Board. It shows the pieces of this folder as cards in columns, one column for each status. **Open Folder dashboard** opens the page that lists a folder's pieces, the same page as the Folder dashboard button on a folder in Files. On a plain folder's page, **Make this folder a vault…** turns it into a vault: it shows what WriteFlex found and saves one settings file in the folder. In any folder, **Switch vault…** opens the menu under the folder's name at the top of Files, the same menu a click on the name opens: it lists your vaults and recent folders, and the one you choose opens in this window. In a vault, **Writing only** hides the folders that hold no writing, and **Show titles in sidebar** lists each work by its title instead of its file name. On the Board, **Show / Hide offcuts** adds a column of offcuts, the passages cut out of pieces and kept, and **Undo last offcut, scrap or source step** takes back the last thing you did to one.

> On the Board, focus a column header and use **Move column left** or **Move column right** to change the column order; dragging a header or choosing Move left or Move right in its menu does the same. On the collection page, **New document** creates a document and **Start a Folio outline** creates a new Folio inside that collection and opens its outline. On a Folio, **Turn back into a collection** asks before removing its Folio declaration and WriteFlex instruction markers; all documents and the Index Note stay.

> On a collection page, **Search this collection** searches the title and text; **Choose collection search scope** changes it to title only. **Filter collection by status**, **Filter collection by purpose**, **Filter collection by tags** and **Filter collection by modified date** open the filters above the table. **Group collection documents** groups the rows by status, purpose or month, and **Sort collection documents** changes their order. **Clear collection search and filters** shows every document again. These controls only change the view.

> **Open Collection menu** opens the menu at the top right. **Make a Folio…** changes this collection into a Folio. **Create index note or edit cover note** opens the existing cover or creates the Index Note, and **Rename this collection…** changes the folder's name.

> **Select all matching collection documents** selects the visible documents. With documents selected, **Set status for selected documents…**, **Move selected documents to…**, **Add selected documents to Folio…** and **Tag selected documents…** open a sheet for that action. **Clear collection selection** removes the selection.

> **The Folio outline.** **Open Folio outline** shows the running order of the parts when a folder is one document. In the Folio outline, <kbd>N</kbd> adds a part and <kbd>A</kbd> adds a file that already exists, and <kbd>F</kbd> adds a title page or other front matter, which exports before the contents page and is never listed in it. **Add all from this folder to the Folio outline** opens a list of every file that can join, in chapter order, which you can reorder with the mouse or <kbd>⌥↑</kbd> and <kbd>⌥↓</kbd> before one Add; **Sort the Folio outline by chapter number** puts the running order in chapter order in one step you can undo. <kbd>⌥↑</kbd> and <kbd>⌥↓</kbd> move the selected part up and down, <kbd>↵</kbd> opens it, or starts it if it is not written yet, and <kbd>⌘⌫</kbd> takes it out of the Folio outline without deleting the file. <kbd>R</kbd> moves to the role menu of the selected part, which is in the Selected part panel beside the running order. A pill beside **Open Folio** tells you whether the agent instructions are written: while one is missing it opens a menu, and the same choices are **Write the agent instructions (AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md)**, **Write AGENTS.md only** and **Write CLAUDE.md only**. The Front matter panel says None yet until there is some; **Choose a file as front matter** offers the files that can go there. <kbd>D</kbd> goes to the Details beside the running order. Type the Folio’s title and standfirst there; <kbd>⇥</kbd> moves to the next field, and <kbd>↓</kbd> opens the picker for Author, Date, Status, Audience or Language, where <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> choose and <kbd>↵</kbd> sets the value. In the calendar, <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> move by day, <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> by week, <kbd>⇞</kbd> and <kbd>⇟</kbd> by month, and <kbd>T</kbd> goes to today. The Details are saved in the Folio’s Index Note, and <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> there takes the last change back. This folder is ten separate lessons, so try the Folio outline in a folder of your own that holds the parts of one piece. On a Folio's page, **Open Folio** (<kbd>⌘↵</kbd>) opens its first part with the Folio part of the Outline beside it, and its menu, or <kbd>⇧⌘↵</kbd>, opens any other part. In a piece that is part of a Folio, a line above the text and a **Folio outline** button in the title bar open the Folio outline, and **Previous piece in Folio** and **Next piece in Folio** move along its running order. In the Folio part of the Outline, drag a piece by its grip to change the running order, or use **Move piece up in running order** and **Move piece down in running order** on the piece that has focus. Files in the folder that are not in the running order are listed on the Folio’s page under Not in the outline, a section you can close or open again with **Show or hide the files not in the outline**; Put back on a file, **Add to the running order** or **Add all to the running order** adds them at the end. **Undo last running-order or section move** takes back the last of these. **Treat folder as one piece / many pieces** sets whether a subfolder counts on the Board as one piece or as each of its files.

> **When the title and the heading differ.** An export names a piece by the title in its metadata, while WriteFlex names it by its heading, so when the two say different things the Folio outline, the Outline and a bar above the text tell you. <kbd>⌃⌥⌘H</kbd> uses the heading as the title and <kbd>⌃⌥⌘T</kbd> uses the title as the heading: on the Folio outline for the selected part, otherwise for the open document. In the Folio outline, <kbd>⌃⌥⇧⌘H</kbd> and <kbd>⌃⌥⇧⌘T</kbd> do the same for every part that differs, in one step. In the document, <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> takes the change back like any edit; from the Folio outline or the Outline, <kbd>⌃⌥⌘Z</kbd> does. <kbd>⌃⌥⌘X</kbd> hides the bar for this document until the title or the heading changes again.

> **The Rail.** <kbd>⌃⌘L</kbd> brings up the Rail, a side list of the collection grouped by status, and the same key puts it away. **Focus rail** moves the keyboard into it. With a group selected in the Rail or on the Board, <kbd>⌃⌥⌘Q</kbd> inserts it here as a table that keeps itself up to date; try it at the end of the line under **Where each part stands**. **Count toward diary / Stop counting toward diary** sets whether the time you spend writing in this folder is recorded in your writing diary.

> **Capture ideas.** Press <kbd>⇧⌘N</kbd> wherever you are, type a title such as **What the waller said about frost**, and press <kbd>↵</kbd>. The idea becomes a new piece in this folder and the caret stays where it was. On the Board, select an offcut and choose **Make idea from offcut** to turn it into an idea in the same way. To keep a table, a quotation or any other find with its sources, open the command palette and choose **Capture to research…**; it is filed in this piece's research folder and can be searched later.

> **Sources.** A source is something you have read for a piece. On the Board, **Show / Hide sources** adds a column of them and **Attach source…** attaches one to the selected piece. **List this piece's sources** lists them for the piece you have open. **Insert reference…** opens your Zotero library as a list to pick from and puts the reference at the caret; **Reference…** in Insert object, under <kbd>⌘K</kbd>, does the same. Both need Zotero on this Mac.

> **Reading Room.** The Reading Room opens a PDF from your Zotero library beside the file, so it needs Zotero and at least one PDF there. <kbd>⌃⌘R</kbd> opens and closes it. <kbd>⌃⌘S</kbd> searches the Shelf, the list of what is in your library. **Reading Room: Document view** shows the PDF and <kbd>⌃⌘H</kbd> shows the highlights you have made in it. **Sync highlights to Zotero** sends those highlights back to Zotero.
