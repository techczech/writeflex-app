# Collect and plan

A folder is a collection, and each file in it is a piece. This file plans the piece about the walk for the parish magazine: its parts, the ideas not yet written and the books behind it. Most of the commands here act on the Board, the Plan or the Reading Room, not on this text, so each instruction says where to be. Where a command has no key, its name is in bold: press <kbd>⇧⌘P</kbd>, type the name and press <kbd>↵</kbd>.

> **Collections, Board and Plan.** Press <kbd>⇧⌘B</kbd> to open the Board. It shows the pieces of this folder as cards in columns, one column for each status. **Open Folder dashboard** opens the page that lists a folder's pieces, the same page as the Folder dashboard button on a folder in Files. On the Board, **Show / Hide offcuts** adds a column of offcuts, the passages cut out of pieces and kept, and **Undo last offcut, scrap or source step** takes back the last thing you did to one.

> **The Plan.** **Open Plan** shows the running order of the parts when a folder is one document. In the Plan, <kbd>N</kbd> adds a part and <kbd>A</kbd> adds a file that already exists. <kbd>⌥↑</kbd> and <kbd>⌥↓</kbd> move the selected part up and down, <kbd>↵</kbd> opens it, or starts it if it is not written yet, and <kbd>⌘⌫</kbd> takes it out of the Plan without deleting the file. This folder is ten separate lessons, so try the Plan in a folder of your own that holds the parts of one piece. In a piece that is part of a Folio, a line above the text and a **Plan** button in the title bar open the Plan, and **Previous piece in Folio** and **Next piece in Folio** move along its running order. In the Folio part of the Outline, drag a piece by its grip to change the running order, or use **Move piece up in running order** and **Move piece down in running order** on the piece that has focus. Files in the folder that are not in the running order are listed under Also in this folder; **Add to the running order** adds one at the end and **Add all to the running order** adds every one that can join. **Undo last running-order or section move** takes back the last of these. **Treat folder as one piece / many pieces** sets whether a subfolder counts on the Board as one piece or as each of its files.

> **The Rail.** <kbd>⌃⌘L</kbd> brings up the Rail, a side list of the collection grouped by status, and the same key puts it away. **Focus rail** moves the keyboard into it. With a group selected in the Rail or on the Board, <kbd>⌃⌥⌘Q</kbd> inserts it here as a table that keeps itself up to date; try it at the end of the line under **Where each part stands**. **Count toward diary / Stop counting toward diary** sets whether the time you spend writing in this folder is recorded in your writing diary.

> **Capture ideas.** Press <kbd>⇧⌘N</kbd> wherever you are, type a title such as **What the waller said about frost**, and press <kbd>↵</kbd>. The idea becomes a new piece in this folder and the caret stays where it was. On the Board, select an offcut and choose **Make idea from offcut** to turn it into an idea in the same way.

> **Sources.** A source is something you have read for a piece. On the Board, **Show / Hide sources** adds a column of them and **Attach source…** attaches one to the selected piece. **List this piece's sources** lists them for the piece you have open. **Insert reference…** opens your Zotero library as a list to pick from and puts the reference at the caret; **Reference…** in Insert object, under <kbd>⌘K</kbd>, does the same. Both need Zotero on this Mac.

## What I have read

A county guide from the 1950s gives the walk as a morning's outing and does not mention the walls. A handbook on dry stone walling explains the through stones and the batter, the lean of each face towards the middle.

> **Reading Room.** The Reading Room opens a PDF from your Zotero library beside the file, so it needs Zotero and at least one PDF there. <kbd>⌃⌘R</kbd> opens and closes it. <kbd>⌃⌘S</kbd> searches the Shelf, the list of what is in your library. **Reading Room: Document view** shows the PDF and <kbd>⌃⌘H</kbd> shows the highlights you have made in it. **Sync highlights to Zotero** sends those highlights back to Zotero.
