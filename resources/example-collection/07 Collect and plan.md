# Collect and plan

A folder is a collection, and each file in it is a piece. This file plans the piece about the walk for the parish magazine: its parts, the ideas not yet written and the books behind it. Most of the commands here act on the Board, the Folio outline or the Reading Room, not on this text, so each instruction says where to be. Where a command has no key, its name is in bold: press <kbd>⇧⌘P</kbd>, type the name and press <kbd>↵</kbd>.

> **Collections, Board and Folio outline.** Press <kbd>⇧⌘B</kbd> to open the Board. It shows the pieces of this folder as cards in columns, one column for each status. For a Folio, <kbd>⇧⌘B</kbd> and the Board button open the workspace's Cards tab grouped by status. **Open Folder dashboard** opens the page that lists a folder's pieces, the same page as the Folder dashboard button on a folder in Files; for a Folio, the folder's list is the Files tab (<kbd>⌥7</kbd>). On a plain folder's page, **Make this folder a vault…** turns it into a vault: it shows what WriteFlex found and saves one settings file in the folder. In any folder, **Switch vault…** opens the menu under the folder's name at the top of Files, the same menu a click on the name opens: it lists your vaults and recent folders, and the one you choose opens in this window. In a vault, **Writing only** hides the folders that hold no writing, and **Show titles in sidebar** lists each work by its title instead of its file name. On the Board, **Show / Hide offcuts** adds a column of offcuts, the passages cut out of pieces and kept, and **Undo last offcut, scrap or source step** takes back the last thing you did to one. For a Folio, the Board commands in this chapter that deal with offcuts, columns and sources work while the **Board** button on the Cards tab's toolbar is on, which shows the full Board inside Cards; with it off, the palette greys them out.

> On the Board, focus a column header and use **Move column left** or **Move column right** to change the column order; dragging a header or choosing Move left or Move right in its menu does the same. On the collection page, **New document** creates a document and **Start a Folio outline** creates a new Folio inside that collection and opens its outline. On a Folio, **Turn back into a collection** asks before removing its Folio declaration and WriteFlex instruction markers; all documents and the Index Note stay.

> On a collection page, **Search this collection** searches the title and text; **Choose collection search scope** changes it to title only. **Filter collection by status**, **Filter collection by purpose**, **Filter collection by tags** and **Filter collection by modified date** open the filters above the table. **Group collection documents** groups the rows by status, purpose or month, and **Sort collection documents** changes their order. **Clear collection search and filters** shows every document again. These controls only change the view.

> **Open Collection menu** opens the menu at the top right. **Make a Folio…** changes this collection into a Folio. **Create index note or edit cover note** opens the existing cover or creates the Index Note, and **Rename this collection…** changes the folder's name.

> **Select all matching collection documents** selects the visible documents. With documents selected, **Set status for selected documents…**, **Move selected documents to…**, **Add selected documents to Folio…** and **Tag selected documents…** open a sheet for that action. **Clear collection selection** removes the selection.

> In a Folio, <kbd>⌥4</kbd> opens the map and <kbd>⌥6</kbd> opens evidence.

> In a Folio, <kbd>⌥5</kbd> opens the Flags view. It gathers flagged passages by part; choose a passage to open its words, or clear its flag while keeping the words.

> **The Folio outline.** **Open Folio outline** shows the running order of the parts when a folder is one document. **Link this Folio's chapters to their files** runs chapter linking again when you ask for it, including after Undo. A Folio opens as one workspace: the binder in the sidebar lists its parts in running order, then the files that are not in the outline, then Research, Notes, Ideas and the Folio bin, and tabs run across the top. A Binder / Inspector switch at the top of the sidebar changes between the two. **Folio workspace: Outline** (<kbd>⌥1</kbd>) lists the parts as rows, **Folio workspace: Text** (<kbd>⌥2</kbd>) shows the selected part's text full width, with buttons for the previous and next part, **Folio workspace: Cards** (<kbd>⌥3</kbd>) shows the cards (Group by status there shows the Board's status lanes: drag a card to a lane, or press <kbd>⌥←</kbd> or <kbd>⌥→</kbd> to move the selected card to the lane on its left or right), and **Folio workspace: Files** (<kbd>⌥7</kbd>) lists the Folio's folder. **Folio workspace: Show or hide the inspector** (<kbd>⌥⌘I</kbd>) swaps the binder for the selected part's synopsis, label, status, priority, role and heading choice, or, with the Folio's title row selected at the top of the binder, for the Folio's details, agent instructions and front matter; the × in the inspector closes it again. For a planned part, the inspector also previews the part's file. **Folio workspace: Text beside the cards** (<kbd>⌥S</kbd>) puts the selected card's text next to the cards: <kbd>↵</kbd> on a card does the same, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> goes back to the cards alone. On the Outline and Cards tabs, <kbd>N</kbd> adds a part. <kbd>A</kbd> adds a file that already exists, and <kbd>F</kbd> adds a title page or other front matter, which exports before the contents page and is never listed in it; both act on the file selected under Not in the outline in the binder, so open that section and focus the file's row first. **Add all from this folder to the Folio outline**, a button on the Outline tab, opens a list of every file that can join, in chapter order, which you can reorder with the mouse or <kbd>⌥↑</kbd> and <kbd>⌥↓</kbd> before one Add; **Sort the Folio outline by chapter number**, also on the Outline tab, puts the running order in chapter order in one step you can undo. Use headings as titles for all is on the Outline tab too. <kbd>⌥↑</kbd> and <kbd>⌥↓</kbd> move the selected part up and down, <kbd>↵</kbd> opens it (beside the cards in Cards, in the Text tab in Outline), or starts it if it is not written yet, and <kbd>⇧↵</kbd> opens the selected part's text in the Text tab. <kbd>Delete</kbd> or <kbd>⌘⌫</kbd> moves the selected part's file to the Folio bin at once and shows Undo (<kbd>⌘Z</kbd>); nothing is deleted, and the bin at the bottom of the binder has a Restore button on each file. <kbd>R</kbd> opens the inspector with the selected part showing and moves to the part's role menu, which is under its synopsis, label, status and priority. A pill in the Folio inspector tells you whether the agent instructions are written. To see it, click the Folio's title row at the top of the binder, or press <kbd>⌥⌘I</kbd> with the title row selected. While one is missing the pill opens a menu, and the same choices are **Write the agent instructions (AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md)**, **Write AGENTS.md only** and **Write CLAUDE.md only**. The Front matter section of the Folio inspector, below the pill and the Details, says None yet until there is some; **Choose a file as front matter** offers the files that can go there. <kbd>D</kbd> opens the Folio inspector and goes to the Details there. Type the Folio’s title and standfirst there; <kbd>⇥</kbd> moves to the next field, and <kbd>↓</kbd> opens the picker for Author, Date, Status, Audience or Language, where <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> choose and <kbd>↵</kbd> sets the value. In the calendar, <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> move by day, <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> by week, <kbd>⇞</kbd> and <kbd>⇟</kbd> by month, and <kbd>T</kbd> goes to today. The Details are saved in the Folio’s Index Note, and <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> there takes the last change back. This folder is ten separate lessons, so try the Folio outline in a folder of your own that holds the parts of one piece. In the workspace header, next to the Folio's name, **Open Folio** (<kbd>⌘↵</kbd>) opens the Folio at its first part in the Text tab, and its Open at… menu, or <kbd>⇧⌘↵</kbd>, lists every written part so you can open any other. In a piece that is part of a Folio, a line above the text and a **Folio outline** button in the title bar open the Folio's workspace, and **Previous piece in Folio** and **Next piece in Folio** move along its running order. Any part of a Folio opened from anywhere, such as the Files sidebar, a search, a link or a tab, opens in the Text tab with that part selected. On the Outline tab, drag a piece by its grip to change the running order, or use **Move piece up in running order** and **Move piece down in running order** on the piece that has focus. Files in the folder that are not in the running order are listed in the binder under Not in the outline, a section with a count that you can open or close again with **Show or hide the files not in the outline**. The + button on a file, **Add to the running order** or **Add all to the running order** adds them at the end. A file that cannot be added shows the reason under it, and its buttons are off. Each file also has the choice "Has its own heading" or "No heading in the text". **Undo last running-order or section move** takes back the last of these. **Treat folder as one piece / many pieces** sets whether a subfolder counts on the Board as one piece or as each of its files.

> **When the title and the heading differ.** An export names a piece by the title in its metadata, while WriteFlex names it by its heading, so when the two say different things the Folio outline, the Outline and a bar above the text tell you. <kbd>⌃⌥⌘H</kbd> uses the heading as the title and <kbd>⌃⌥⌘T</kbd> uses the title as the heading: on the Folio outline for the selected part, otherwise for the open document. In the Folio outline, <kbd>⌃⌥⇧⌘H</kbd> and <kbd>⌃⌥⇧⌘T</kbd> do the same for every part that differs, in one step. In the document, <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> takes the change back like any edit; from the Folio outline or the Outline, <kbd>⌃⌥⌘Z</kbd> does. <kbd>⌃⌥⌘X</kbd> hides the bar for this document until the title or the heading changes again.

> **The Rail.** <kbd>⌃⌘L</kbd> brings up the Rail, a side list of the collection grouped by status, and the same key puts it away. **Focus rail** moves the keyboard into it. With a group selected in the Rail or on the Board, <kbd>⌃⌥⌘Q</kbd> inserts it here as a table that keeps itself up to date; try it at the end of the line under **Where each part stands**. **Count toward diary / Stop counting toward diary** sets whether the time you spend writing in this folder is recorded in your writing diary.

## Gather evidence and try a map

In a Folio, **Evidence** (<kbd>⌥6</kbd>) shows which parts have sources. Choose a part and use **Add evidence**, or drag a file, a Zotero item or a highlight onto its card. A source can be attached to a planned part before you start writing it. Your notes and attachments are kept in the Folio’s evidence-and-notes Markdown file.

For an available quotation, **Insert with citation** opens the part’s text and inserts the passage with its source. Zotero quotations are read from Zotero when you need them. A highlight waiting to reach Zotero attaches its parent item until the quotation is available.

**Map** (<kbd>⌥4</kbd>) lets you arrange parts, sources, notes and text cards. Start with **Put all parts on the map**, then move cards, add labelled clusters and draw arrows. Choose **Path**, pick parts in the order you want to try, then **Finish path**. **Use this order** previews the changes; **Write this order** updates the running order. Parts outside the path keep their places. You can keep the map without changing the running order.

The map is a JSON Canvas file in the Folio folder. Its Undo reverses map edits; the running order has its own Undo.
