# WriteFlex 0.28.0-alpha.21

## More room to write

Versions, Agent and Research now share the right sidebar. It starts closed the first time after this update, then remembers your choice. Agent notes are in Agent, and Archive is part of Research. The left Outline shows one tree, and Manage Folio calls its outline view Table.

The Scratchpad has a status-bar chip showing how many candidates it holds. Open it to work beside your text in the right sidebar, or choose Float to keep it separate. Existing floating layouts stay floating.

## Clearer editing and versions

Use **Track my changes** as one on/off toggle, and **Accept** or **Decline** agent proposals in the review margin. In Versions, **Save version** asks whether you want to save this piece or the whole Folio. Folio contents show how many parts are written and make unwritten parts clear.

Settings opens at the top, metadata has more room to edit, and the archive footer wraps in a narrow sidebar. Status-bar controls disappear whole when space is tight, and hidden controls no longer interrupt keyboard navigation. Saved shortcut history still recognises renamed commands.

## Pictures and phone reading

Pictures beside a document now appear in the preview even when the document is inside a nested folder. On phones, exported pages have a **Contents** button you can open and close. Choosing a heading closes it so you can read the text; Escape closes it too.
