# WriteFlex 0.28.0-alpha.17

## Open comments

When you share a piece or Folio, choose Private comments or Open comments for each link, and change it later on the Sharing page or in the Shares list. With open comments, every reader of that link sees the others' comments with their names and can reply to them. Their replies arrive in your margin inside the thread they answer. Comments written while a link was private stay private after you open it, and so do your replies.

## Sharing works the same from any of your Macs

A shared piece or Folio now shows as shared on every Mac where you open the same workspace and have set up sharing on the same Cloudflare account. Any of them can publish your saves to the link, receive readers' comments and proposed edits, and handle them. An item handled on one Mac shows as handled on the others. Older text never replaces newer: a Mac that has not yet received your latest file waits for it.

A copy of a shared folder never takes over the link. If a Mac finds two folders that claim the same identity, both wait until you choose the original on the Sharing page.

After installing, press Update in Settings › Sharing once, then open WriteFlex on the Mac that made each existing link, so the link's record moves to your sharing service.
