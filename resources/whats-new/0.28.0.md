# WriteFlex 0.28.0

- Share pieces and Folios by link on your own Cloudflare account, with reader comments, proposed edits and discussion.
- Keep cuttings, pictures and sources in the Scratchpad; compare candidates and put text back with Undo.
- Plan Folios with Cards, Table, Map, Flags, Text and Evidence views; save and compare whole-Folio versions.

## Writing and reviewing

Review cards sit beside their passages, with filters and bulk actions in the status bar. Track my changes records your own edits. Versions, Agent and Research share the Right Sidebar, which starts closed after this update and then remembers your choice. Open the Scratchpad from its status-bar chip, or choose Float. Plan is now the Folio outline, Notes are Comments, and the earlier outline view in Manage Folio is Table.

## Working with a reviewer

Export a saved Folio version and record who received it. Import the reviewer's returned Word file as an incoming version, confirm its part matches and review its changes as suggestions. Where you both changed a paragraph, read the sent, reviewer and current text side by side before deciding. Inserted CSV, image and widget files have their own versions.

## Files, history and agents

Make a vault to organise writing and pinned work. Metadata has labelled fields. Merge helps when a file changes on disk. OneDrive writes go into local recovery first, and WriteFlex reconciles only copies it can prove are identical or superseded. GitHub sign-in and Git backup are available. WriteFlex can install its command and skill for the agents you choose. Zotero highlights are sent through the WriteFlex for Zotero plugin while Zotero is open.

History is kept forever by default. The diary glance shows today's words, and saved diary queries work across workspaces. Check for Updates reports a newer release and links to its download. It installs nothing.

## Export

Exports use the declared title and the Folio's running order. Phone pages have a Contents button. Exported HTML includes one small menu script, and the contents links also work without it. Pictures in nested folders show in the preview, and sidebar dragging keeps at least 55% of the width for the editor.
