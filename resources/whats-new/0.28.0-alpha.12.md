# WriteFlex 0.28.0-alpha.12

## See what changed since your last Folio version

Inside a Folio, a dot marks each part you have changed since your newest saved Folio version. The status bar says how many parts changed and turns green once you save a version. The Versions tab now starts with your working draft, and the side panel shows the history of the part under your cursor.

## Read an old Folio version

In the Versions tab, choose Open read-only (or double-click a version). The Folio opens as it was saved, with a banner naming the version, in Outline, Text and Cards. Nothing can be changed while it is open. Press Esc or Back to working draft to return.

## Also in this build

Save a whole Folio with Shift-Command-S, and the Versions tab (Option-8), both from alpha.11.
