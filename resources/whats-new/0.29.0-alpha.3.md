# WriteFlex 0.29.0-alpha.3

- Quitting no longer stalls after the window closes.
- Folios are listed by their own titles, with a Folios section in the sidebar, Open a Folio…, and one place for all of a Folio's comments.
- Match system appearance follows your Mac's light or dark mode, in Settings too.
- The window controls no longer crowd the Mac's window buttons.
- Renaming, moving, Quick Open and opening WriteFlex are faster in large vaults.
- The first shortcut you press after launch now works.
- A Git vault can show whether its backup reached its remote copy.

## Quitting

⌘Q used to leave WriteFlex busy for several seconds after its window had closed, longer on a busy Mac. Closing a window now stops all of a Collection's file watching in one step, so WriteFlex quits straight away.

## Folios

A Folio is now named by its own title everywhere it is listed: the sidebar, search, Quick Open, the Manage Folio button and the Folio line under the text. Its folder appears as a second line. A Folios section at the top of the sidebar lists every Folio in the vault; you can fold it away. Choose Open a Folio… from the command palette or the Collect menu to pick one by title, and Quick Open (⇧⌘K) lists matching Folios first.

All comments in this Folio (command palette, Review menu) opens a Comments tab with every waiting comment and suggested edit across the Folio's parts, grouped by part in running order. Click one to open its part with that comment selected.

## Appearance

Appearance has a third choice, Match system. The editor and the Settings window follow your Mac's light or dark mode as it changes. The editor, distraction-free mode and Settings keep clear of the window buttons in the top corner, and the toolbar controls line up.

## Speed

In large vaults, renaming and moving documents and folders, Quick Open, and starting WriteFlex are all faster. At launch your last document opens before WriteFlex has finished looking through the whole vault.

## Backup status

If you back up a vault kept in Git with a tool that keeps its own verification records, set Settings › Advanced › Backup › Backup tool records to that tool's settings folder. The status bar and the Sync tab then say whether this folder's last upload was verified, and warn when newer writing has not been uploaded yet or when Git ignore rules leave writing out. Saving in WriteFlex is not a backup, and the status never turns green because of a save.

## Smaller fixes

- The first ⇧⌘K or ⇧⌘P pressed right after launch is no longer lost.
- Moving a document keeps relative paths in its front matter (such as a cover image) working.
- Repairing links after a rename no longer shows a changed-on-disk notice over your typing.
- The status bar says "Saved on this Mac (not a backup)", so a save is not mistaken for a backup.

## Vault sync stays off

Sending a vault's changes through Git between Macs is in this version but switched off by default. It is experimental and has not yet been proven between two Macs. Settings › Advanced has a switch for testing it, which takes effect the next time WriteFlex starts; leave it off unless you are testing.
