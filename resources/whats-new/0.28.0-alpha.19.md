# WriteFlex 0.28.0-alpha.19

## Live comments on shared links

When readers have your shared page open, new comments and your replies now appear by themselves within about ten seconds. When you publish a new save, their page updates in place and keeps their reading position. If they are in the middle of writing a comment, nothing moves: the page offers **Update now** instead.

Your Macs also pick up new comments every 15 seconds while WriteFlex is in front.

To turn this on, open **Settings › Sharing** and press **Update sharing service** once.
