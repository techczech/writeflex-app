# WriteFlex 0.28.0-alpha.15

## Bring a reviewer's Word file back into a Folio

In the Versions tab, choose Import revision and pick the Word file someone returned. WriteFlex matches its sections to your parts and asks you about any it is not sure of. It keeps the file as an incoming version from that person, linked to what you sent them.

## Her changes arrive as suggestions

Her tracked changes (or, without tracked changes, what differs from the version you sent) appear in the margin of each part, with her name on them. Your text is untouched until you accept a suggestion. A passage of hers that no longer fits your current text is listed instead of being placed.

## Decide, then save

The Decide step lists every part with suggestions and every change to the structure: a new section to add as a part, a part she moved, a part she took out. You decide each one; a progress count shows how far you are. When everything is decided, WriteFlex offers to save a version called "After her revision".

Agents can import and withdraw a revision, but only you confirm matches and decide changes.
